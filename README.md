# final_project2026
# Description:
This project is a social music review platform designed for music listeners to rate, review, discover, and share their opinions about songs and albums. The application will allow users to create profiles, keep track of music they have listened to, and interact with other users. 

Users will be able to browse a music catalog, write reviews, give ratings, follow other users, and explore a social feed featuring music reviews and recommendations. The goal is to create a community where users can discover new music, exchange opinions, and connect with others who share their musical interests.
